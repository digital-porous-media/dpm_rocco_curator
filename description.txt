This dataset contains stereolithography (STL) files that are ready for 3D printing of a hand-held game. The game's design was inspired by microfluidic chips and the classic 'ball maze game,' in which the player must guide a metal ball from one point to another through a labyrinth, and it is using an image from Digital Porous Media Portal. This provides a player with the idea how tortuous paths throw a porous medium such as soil and rocks can be, and how both fluids and particles that travel in subsurface can get stuck in the pore spaces. A game was originally in part developed and tested during GEOFORCE 2025 high school camp. Since then, the quality and size of the print were further improved based on testing it by different users at The University of Texas at Austin and the Python-based workflow has been developed.  It is intended to be of wide interest to school children, university students, teachers and researchers of porous media alike.
